*confirmed* — observed in a source during this review and unchanged (method and date on the claim). *corroborated* — coordinates only: two earlier sources (the previous edition's geocode and the CMS point) agree, but neither records its match type, so the point is not verified to the campus. *established* — the previous edition had nothing (unknown, or a town with no street) and this review documented a value. *corrected* — this review changed a value the previous edition stated to another documented value (`changedFrom` keeps the old one). *carried-forward* — taken from the previous edition with that edition's date, not re-observed. *conflicting* — credible sources disagree. *searched-unresolved* — searched in this review (method, date and what was found are on the claim), but what was found does not settle it, so the claim is unknown; this includes a claim whose earlier documented value this review could not confirm (the earlier value stays in `changedFrom` and is quoted in the note), which is never counted as corrected. *inaccessible* — the needed source could not be reached. *not-assessed* — no evidence either way, and no search recorded. *missing* — no claim recorded; for `location`, a record deliberately left unlocated (its note says why).

Review completeness and claim verification are different measures: a claim can be reviewed and still be unknown.

## Capability searches: what "no record found" covers

The last October 2026 capability pass searched national certification, thrombectomy, 24/7 thrombectomy and state designation for 117 targeted records (plus the two new Wyoming records) that the earlier passes had left partly or not assessed. Every finding rests on web-search extracts: the Joint Commission Quality Check, the DNV directory and most state pages could not be opened. A `no-record-found` therefore means "not found in the sources named in the claim's note". Every national certification, thrombectomy and 24/7 thrombectomy `no-record-found` note set in this review names those sources with their dates, and the October 2026 search, and says the issuer directories were not reached; a state-designation `no-record-found` note names instead the state roster it was read against (DOH 345-299, the Idaho TSE roster) or the absence of a state program (Montana, Alaska) or of a public roster (Wyoming, where it rests on the designation rule). Claims carried forward from the previous edition keep that edition's wording and date (`editionAsOf`).
