*confirmed* — observed in a source during this review and unchanged (method and date on the claim). *corroborated* — coordinates only: two earlier sources (the previous edition's geocode and the CMS point) agree, but neither records its match type, so the point is not verified to the campus. *established* — the previous edition had nothing (unknown, or a town with no street) and this review documented a value. *corrected* — this review changed a value the previous edition stated (`changedFrom` keeps the old one). *carried-forward* — taken from the previous edition with that edition's date, not re-observed. *conflicting* — credible sources disagree. *inaccessible* — the needed source could not be reached. *not-assessed* — no evidence either way.

Review completeness and claim verification are different measures: a claim can be reviewed and still be unknown.
